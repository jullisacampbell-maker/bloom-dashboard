Bloom Family Tech Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Overview

Bloom Family Tech is built by Bloom Technologies to help families organize homeschool resources, home life, planning, meals, goals, and routines.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide, including your email address (for billing or support), profile information, planning notes, meal notes, family organization details, and support messages.

How we use information

We use your information to provide Bloom Family Tech and Bloom Home, improve the product, provide support, and manage subscriptions.

How your data is stored

Bloom currently saves your planning data, profile, and preferences in your browser using local storage on the device you use. This means your data stays on that device unless you export a backup. We do not currently store your planning data in a cloud database for the public Bloom app.

Export and backup

You can export a backup of your Bloom Profile and data from the Profile tab in Bloom Home at any time. We recommend doing this before clearing browser storage or switching devices.

Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. Bloom does not store your full payment card details.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers to operate the app, process payments, or provide support.

Your choices

You may contact support to request help with your account or data.

Contact

Questions? Email bloomfamilytech@gmail.com.
