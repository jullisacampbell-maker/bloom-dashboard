Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for Bloom OS users.

What is Bloom OS?

Bloom OS is the family operating system — one calm place for home, meals, routines, goals, and family life. Plan your day, week, meals, home rhythm, goals, and more in one dashboard.

Is Bloom OS free?

Yes. The Free tier is a useful starting point for organizing family life. Pro and Family plans unlock additional planning, personalization, and family features.

Does my data save?

Yes. Bloom currently saves your information in this browser using local storage. Your data stays available when you return on the same device and browser. Use Export Backup in your Profile tab to protect your data before clearing browser storage or moving to another device.

Can I use Bloom on another device?

Your data is stored in the browser you use. To move to another device, export a backup from your Profile tab and import it on the new device. Bloom does not currently sync data automatically across devices.

How do I upgrade?

Choose Pro or Family from the upgrade screen in the app. Paid plan access is managed through Stripe.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel a subscription anytime. Access continues through the end of your billing period.
