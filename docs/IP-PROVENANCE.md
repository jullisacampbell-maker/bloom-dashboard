## 3. Core Product Thesis

Bloom Technologies is being built around the belief that families do not necessarily need more disconnected tools. They need technology that helps existing tools, routines, responsibilities, schedules, learning resources, meals, movement, and goals work together.

The product direction is based on lived family workflow rather than generic productivity assumptions.

The approved family-technology design principle is:

**Navy = structure / technology**  
**Green = growth / positive action**  
**Cream = family warmth**

- prioritization methods;
- orchestration logic;
- conflict-resolution logic;
- household scoring;
- adaptation rules;
- internal prompts;
- proprietary data structures;
- unreleased algorithms;
- technical methods that may later be treated as confidential, trade-secret, or patent-sensitive material.
